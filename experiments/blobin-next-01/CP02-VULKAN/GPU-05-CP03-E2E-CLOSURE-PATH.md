## Key boundary conditions

- **Neither the existing 2D marker nor the optical ROI is a physical sensor.** A screenshot and `wp_presentation` receipts are not photons.
- `cavity-control` is a 2D semantic input control that affects a separate 3D SDF parameter. Its visible overlay must not silently be called geometric picking against the animated 3D cavity.
- The current ROI lights on *any eligible pointer press*. It MUST NOT be described as proof that the semantic scene caused the light until E2E-02 qualifies that exact chain.
- Physical input-to-photon certification cannot be performed without suitable external switch and photodiode hardware, calibration, trial provenance and an independent observer. The software alone cannot close this gate.
- The existing 120s cadence packet cannot be retroactively transformed into a full CP03 E2E packet by appending a later screenshot or other-run interaction.
- CP02 (geometry), CP07 (alpha), CP08 (cross-vendor), and CUDA-05F supervisory authority are distinct programs; apply only the dependencies actually required by the ratified CP03 contract, and never inherit a PASS implicitly.
